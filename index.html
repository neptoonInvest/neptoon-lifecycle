WHAT IS A LIFE CYCLE MODEL?

A map of your financial life — not a ledger.

A Life Cycle model connects the financial decisions you make today with possible resources and consumption many years into the future. The purpose is not to predict your life, but to make better savings and consumption decisions today.

THE LIFE CYCLE PRINCIPLE

Four phases. One financial decision.

From building wealth to financial freedom, consumption and legacy. Neptoon connects your entire financial life in one model.

01 · BEFORE n*
Building Wealth
Savings and returns grow your total economic resources.
02 · AT n*
Financial Freedom Day
The earliest date your desired consumption and legacy can be financed.
03 · AFTER n*
Using Wealth
Your resources finance consumption throughout the rest of your life.
04 · END OF LIFE
Legacy
Your remaining wealth is measured against your desired legacy.
You choose consumption and legacy. Neptoon minimizes the time to financial freedom.

Real estate
Current household property wealth.
Mortgage loans and other liabilities
Liabilities are treated differently depending on whether they are explicitly included in the Life Cycle model.
A · Mortgage loans
Debt attached to the property is explicitly modeled. Principal repayment is a balance-sheet substitution; interest is not deducted from household cash flow.
—
B · Bank Loans
Standalone bank debt, including housing or personal loans. Repayment reduces outstanding liabilities without being linked to asset liquidation. Interest is not deducted from household cash flow.
C · Deferred pension tax
Optional estimated tax liability on existing pension wealth. Deducted once at the pension end date without affecting pension accumulation.
D · Other liabilities
Assets may carry liabilities that are not explicitly modeled. These can include deferred taxes, private debt and other obligations associated with an asset.
D1
Deferred taxes Pension wealth, business interests and other assets may contain future or deferred tax obligations.
D2
Other debt and obligations Liabilities not explicitly entered in the model are not deducted from the modeled asset value.
D3
Serviced outside the model These obligations are assumed to be serviced from income or other resources outside the Life Cycle model.
Mortgage debt is the exception: when included above, it is explicitly incorporated into the Life Cycle calculation.
